Method & Legal Notes
Scope Aggregate VPN provider footprint only. Tor, proxy, and relay records are excluded from this public atlas.
No raw configs This page does not redistribute VPN configuration files, client assets, credentials, tokens, or raw provider responses.
Interpretation Reported country and ASN values are infrastructure context signals. Reported country may come from provider metadata, source labels, filename heuristics, or Geo MMDB fallback. It is not provider ownership, company jurisdiction, affiliation, abuse, or malicious activity.
